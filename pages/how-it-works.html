Questions people ask

Is this a scam? Why would a settlement pay me?Class action settlements are real court cases. When a company settles, the court approves a fund for everyone affected, and anyone who qualifies can claim a share. Most people never hear about them, so the money goes unclaimed.
Do I need proof?Many settlements need no proof. When one does, we tell you exactly what to send before we file.
Why not just file myself?You can, for free. Claimclub saves you from finding every case, reading every notice and filling in every form.
What do you do with my information?We put it on the claim forms you agree to and send it to each settlement administrator. We never sell it.
