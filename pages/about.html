ABOUT CLAIMCLUB

Money you're owed shouldn't go unclaimed

Every year, settlement money goes unclaimed because people never hear about the case or never get to the forms. We built Claimclub to find those settlements for you and do the paperwork.
143open nationwide settlements we track
Dailychecks for new cases and changed deadlines
100%of every payout goes to you, never a cut

What we believe

100% of every payoutWe charge a flat price, never a cut of what you get.
Free to file yourselfEvery settlement page links to its official site, where filing is free.
Your details stay privateWe only send your info to the administrator of a claim you agree to file.
Cancel anytimeOne tap in your account. Claims we already filed stay filed.
What we are notClaimclub is a claims preparation service. We are not a law firm, a settlement administrator or class counsel, and we are not tied to the companies or courts in the cases we list.
