Money you're owed shouldn't go unclaimed.

Every year, billions of dollars from class action settlements go unclaimed, mostly because people never hear about them or don't have time for the forms. Claimclub fixes that.

How Claimclub works

1. Check for freeAnswer a few quick questions and we match you to open settlements and show what you could be owed.
2. Sign onceWe pre-fill every claim form from your info. You review and e-sign each one.
3. We file and trackWe submit your claims and track each one until the settlement pays you directly.

What we believe

  • Your payout is yours. We charge a flat filing fee or a membership, never a percentage of your settlement.
  • No pressure. Checking is free, and you can always file any claim yourself, free, on its official settlement site. We link to every one.
  • Honest numbers. Estimates come straight from court notices, and we tell you when a payout depends on documents or is shared pro rata.
  • Privacy first. We only share your details with the administrator of a claim you sign for, and we never sell your data.

What we're not

Claimclub is a claims preparation service. We are not a law firm, a settlement administrator or class counsel, and we are not affiliated with the companies or courts involved in the settlements we list. Some arbitration claims are handled by independent partner law firms, and we will always tell you before a referral.

Where our settlements come from

We track court-approved settlements from public court filings, official settlement websites and administrator notices, and check them every week for new claims and deadline changes. Members also get scans for state and local settlements near them.
