Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

We collect what we need to find and file your claims. We share it with settlement administrators only to file the claims you sign for. We do not sell your personal information. You can see, download or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how DBC Workshop LLC, doing business as Claimclub ("Claimclub," "we," "us"), collects, uses and shares information when you use claimclub.io and the Claimclub app.

3. How we use information

  • Match you to settlements and estimate what you may be owed;
  • Prepare and submit the claims you sign for, and track them;
  • Send deadline, new-match and payout alerts you have turned on;
  • Process payments, prevent fraud and keep the Service secure;
  • Provide support and improve the Service; and
  • Comply with law.

4. How we share information

  • Settlement administrators, only for the claims you sign for and only the details that claim requires.
  • Partner law firms, only for arbitration claims you choose to pursue, and only after you agree to the referral.
  • Service providers that help us run the Service (hosting, payments, email and SMS, bank-data connection, analytics), under contracts that limit their use of your data.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law or to protect rights, safety and the integrity of the claims process.
  • Business transfers: if Claimclub is involved in a merger or sale, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

Meta Pixel, Google Ads and TikTok. Our website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms, Inc. that tells us when people visit our pages after seeing one of our ads, so we can measure and improve our advertising. It can collect the pages you visit, your browser, IP address and a cookie ID, and Meta may use this under its own privacy policy. Under some state privacy laws this can count as sharing for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising. We do not send Meta the answers you give in the claim flow, your saved details, your claims or your signed forms. When someone starts or pays for a membership trial, our server also tells Meta about that purchase through Meta's Conversions API, with the amount, the time, the page address, your IP address and browser, the Meta cookie IDs, and your email address in hashed (scrambled, one-way) form, so Meta can match it to the ad you came from. We also use the Google Ads tag from Google LLC, which tells us when someone who clicked one of our Google ads starts a membership trial, so we can measure those ads. It can collect the pages you visit, your browser, IP address and a cookie ID with the ad click ID, and Google may use this under its own privacy policy. It never receives your answers, details, claims or signed forms. We also use the TikTok Pixel from TikTok, which tells us when people visit our pages after seeing one of our TikTok ads. It can collect the pages you visit, your browser, IP address and a cookie ID, and TikTok may use this under its own privacy policy. It never receives your answers, details, claims or signed forms. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we do not load the pixel, the Google Ads tag or the TikTok Pixel and we do not send these server events. You can also opt out by emailing privacy@mail.claimclub.io.

5. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your saved information in the app, turn notifications on or off in Settings, and disconnect your bank at any time. Depending on where you live (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states with privacy laws), you may have the right to know what we collect, access or get a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, and opt out of sale (we do not sell your data) or of targeted advertising, including the Meta Pixel, Google Ads tag and TikTok Pixel described in section 4. To make a request, email privacy@mail.claimclub.io. We will verify your identity and respond within the time the law requires, and we will not discriminate against you for using these rights. Deleting your account does not withdraw claims already submitted to administrators.

6. Retention

We keep claim records and signatures for as long as your claims are open and for up to 7 years after, so we can answer administrator questions and show proof of your authorization. Other data is deleted or anonymized when no longer needed or when you ask us to delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and monitoring to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us about any suspicious activity.

8. Children

The Service is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We will post updates here and tell you about material changes before they take effect.
