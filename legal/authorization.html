Claim Filing Authorization Agreement

Effective November 13, 2026

This agreement lets Claimclub prepare and submit claim forms on your behalf for the settlements you choose. You sign it once for each claim, inside the app, before anything is filed. Settlement money is always paid directly to you by the settlement administrator. Claimclub never receives, holds or takes a share of your payout.

1. Parties

This Claim Filing Authorization Agreement (the "Authorization") is between you, the person identified in your Claimclub account ("you" or the "Claimant"), and [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], doing business as Claimclub ("Claimclub," "we" or "us").

2. What you authorize

For each settlement you select and sign for in the Claimclub app (each, a "Covered Claim"), you appoint Claimclub as your authorized agent, for the limited purpose of:

  1. Preparing the claim form using the information you provide to us, including your name, contact details, mailing address, date of birth and your answers to eligibility questions;
  2. Submitting the claim form to the settlement administrator through the administrator's official claim website, mail or other method the administrator accepts, including uploading any documents you choose to provide;
  3. Receiving confirmations, claim IDs and status updates from the administrator about the Covered Claim and showing them to you in your dashboard; and
  4. Responding to simple administrative requests from the administrator (for example, confirming a mailing address), only with information you have already given us or approve.

3. What you do not authorize

  • Claimclub may not receive, deposit, endorse or redirect any settlement payment. All payments go from the administrator to you using the payment method you choose on the claim form.
  • Claimclub may not make statements on your behalf that you have not confirmed, opt you out of a settlement, object to a settlement, or release any rights beyond those released by filing the claim itself.
  • Claimclub may not file any claim you did not select and sign for.
  • This Authorization does not create an attorney-client relationship, and Claimclub does not give legal advice.

4. Your statements and signature

Most claim forms ask you to confirm, often under penalty of perjury, that the information is true and that you meet the eligibility requirements. By signing a Covered Claim in the app you confirm that:

  1. All information you provided is true, complete and your own;
  2. You believe in good faith that you meet the eligibility requirements described for that settlement; and
  3. You authorize Claimclub to apply your electronic signature to that claim form exactly as presented to you.

Submitting a false claim can be a crime and can lead to your claim being denied. If you are unsure whether you qualify, do not sign for that claim. Read the official settlement notice, which we link from every claim.

5. Electronic signatures and records

You agree to sign this Authorization and each claim electronically under the federal Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (E-SIGN) and applicable state law. Your electronic signature has the same effect as a handwritten signature. We keep a record of each signature with the date, time and the claim it applies to, and you can request a copy at any time.

6. Settlements that must be filed elsewhere

Some settlements only accept claims filed by the class member personally, require documents we cannot obtain, or pay eligible people automatically. For those, we will tell you in the app and either guide you through filing yourself or mark the claim as automatic. Some arbitration claims are handled by independent partner law firms. Before any such referral, we will tell you who the firm is and you will sign that firm's own engagement agreement. Claimclub does not share in any legal fees.

7. No guarantee of payment

Courts and settlement administrators decide eligibility, approve or deny claims, and set payment amounts and timing. Amounts shown in Claimclub are estimates or caps taken from settlement notices. Payments can be reduced pro rata, delayed by appeals, or not made at all. Claimclub does not guarantee that any claim will be approved or paid.

8. Fees

Claimclub charges for its preparation and filing service only, either a one-time filing fee or a membership, as described in the Terms & Conditions. Claimclub never charges a percentage of your settlement payment. Filing a claim directly with the administrator is always free.

9. Duration and revocation

This Authorization lasts for each Covered Claim until the claim is finally paid, denied or withdrawn. You can revoke it for any claim that has not yet been submitted at any time, in the app or by emailing support@claimclub.io. Revoking it does not withdraw a claim that has already been submitted. If you want to withdraw a submitted claim, we will tell you how to contact the administrator.

10. Privacy

We share your information with a settlement administrator only to file and support your Covered Claims. See our Privacy Policy.
